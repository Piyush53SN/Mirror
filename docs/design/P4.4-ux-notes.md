# P4.4-UX design notes (Tier D, docs only)

Status: implemented on `arena/46c54cde-mirror`, **not tagged**, no version bump in this pass.
Scope is the small pass between P4.4a and P4.4b. P4.4b (App rules) and P4.4c (Intent Check) are not started.

## Principles that hold for every item

- **Tier D only.** Mirror reads OS usage and the user's own labels. It never guesses intent. Duration never implies intent.
- **No guilt, no streaks, no red.** Copy is neutral. Soft budgets read `"12m over 45m today"`.
- **Nothing is written on render.** Every write is a tap: Accept, Save, Turn on, Delete.
- **Partial data is labelled, never extrapolated.** Weeks with fewer than 7 recorded days are marked partial.

## 1. Home weekly summary and one-time reminder offer

- `WeeklySummaryCard` (Home, under the budget strip) reuses the weekly-report engine. It shows the 7-day total, the top app, and a change line only when the engine's `hasPriorWeekComparison` gate passes (both weeks have 7 recorded days and at least 14 distinct days). Otherwise it shows the engine's own reason.
- Tapping the card opens the full Weekly Report sheet.
- **One-time offer:** `reminderOfferSeen` (default `false`, sealed `weekly_report` prefs). The offer asks once, says reminders are off by default, and states that quiet hours (23:00–05:00) are respected.
  - **Turn on** goes through the existing opt-in path. `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is requested only on that tap. A denial keeps reminders off and shows a notice.
  - **Not now** records the answer. Reminders stay off.
- The offer is hidden once answered or once any reminder is on.

## 2. Budget suggestions: up to three

- `suggestBudgets()` returns up to `BUDGET_SUGGESTION_MAX_ITEMS` (3), strongest average first. The thresholds are unchanged: 3 recorded days, at least 30 minutes, rounded to 15 minutes, skip budgeted, excluded, and dismissed apps.
- The Weekly Report card shows **Accept all (N)** when there are two or more, plus per-item **Accept** and **Dismiss**.
- Accept all runs the same single-budget accept path once per suggestion, in order. A partial failure is reported honestly as "N added. M could not be saved."
- Dismiss is per app and lasts 7 days (sealed `budget_suggestions_v1`).

## 3. Layout consolidation (no behaviour or storage change)

- **Settings** (`SettingsSheet`, opened from `You` → *Open Settings*) holds:
  - `ReminderSettings`: both reminder toggles, weekday and time, and **the single reminder quiet-hours control** (`quietHoursStartHour/EndHour`).
  - `LocalDataControls`: export JSON, clear labels, typed `DELETE`, and the reset audit.
- **Insights:** the Weekly Report entry stays at the top. What-if, goals, score parts, curiosity, and depth collapse under **Advanced** (collapsed by default). Advanced is one toggle.
- Quiet hours have exactly one control. The What-if "Quiet hours" *scenario* is a separate estimate and is not a setting.

## 4. Trend labels need 14 recorded days

- Below `FULL_EVOLUTION_DAYS` (14) recorded days, a row's trend is `not_enough_history` and its label is **"Not enough history yet"**. Growing and fading are never shown below 14 days, and the summary line drops the growing/fading counts.
- At 14 or more days the labels are `growing`, `steady`, or `fading`, as before.

## 5. Small fixes

- **Goal chip wrap:** `Pill` uses `minHeight`, so a wrapped label grows the chip instead of being clipped (`"App time / day"`).
- **Bottom nav:** one geometry source (`tabBarMetrics`). The solid canvas band reaches past the top edge of the bar and always covers the system navigation inset. The bar, its backing, and the scrim share one bottom offset. **Needs a device check.**
- **What-if chips:** `Pill` takes `selected`, which is exposed to TalkBack. The visual state is the existing primary fill when on.
- **No Label on system or utility apps:** Gallery and media viewers are in the labeling exclusion list, so Sessions shows no Label pill for them. Google Photos is not on the list (see open questions).
